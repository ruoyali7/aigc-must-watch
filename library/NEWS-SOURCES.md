# AI News Sources

Maintain a current discovery list for AI news candidates.

## Primary Sources
- official company blogs / newsrooms
- product release notes
- model documentation
- research papers / labs
- official demos and creator announcements

## Secondary Discovery Sources
- Hacker News
- Reddit
- Product Hunt
- X / creator posts
- YouTube AI channels
- AI newsletters
- technology publications

## News Candidate Record
For each story, store:
- headline
- date / time
- primary source URL
- secondary coverage if useful
- one-sentence factual summary
- why it matters
- possible visual metaphor
- meme / humor potential
- uncertainty or verification notes

## Rule
Use discovery sources to find stories, but verify important factual claims against primary or high-quality reporting before scripting.
