# AI Film Sources

Maintain a current list of places used to discover AI-generated films, animation, ads, music videos, experimental video, and creators.

## Competition / Festival Sources
- Runway AI Film Festival
- Reply AI Film Festival
- Project Odyssey
- Curious Refuge competitions and showcases

## Creator / Distribution Platforms
- YouTube
- Vimeo
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels

## Discovery Rules

For every candidate, record:
- original creator
- original URL when possible
- publication date
- film length
- category
- verified tools if disclosed
- why it may be worth reviewing
- strong timestamps after viewing

Prefer the creator’s original upload over reposts or aggregators.
