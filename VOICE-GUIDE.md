# Voice Guide

## Voice Identity

English narration should sound like a smart friend who follows AI closely and has taste.

Core traits:
- conversational
- fast but clear
- lightly sarcastic
- curious
- visually descriptive
- willing to call out hype

Avoid:
- corporate PR tone
- fake excitement
- generic “revolutionary” language
- long technical definitions
- dense sentences that sound written rather than spoken

## Writing for TTS

Prefer short spoken lines.

Use pauses and conversational connectors:
- Okay.
- Yeah.
- Except...
- Here’s the weird part.
- And somehow...
- Which sounds great, until...

## Hook Style

Good hooks usually start with tension, surprise, or a strong claim.

Examples:
- “Okay, this is ridiculous.”
- “Google had exactly three days to enjoy being number one.”
- “This might be the best AI film I’ve seen all week.”
- “Someone actually let an AI do this.”

Avoid opening with:
- “Today, Company X announced...”
- “In recent AI news...”
- “Artificial intelligence continues to evolve...”

## Humor Rule

The joke should clarify or amplify the underlying story, not replace the facts.

Use humor from:
- rivalry
- status reversal
- absurd scale
- relatable workplace dynamics
- overconfidence
- awkward failure
- unexpected success

## Opinion Rule

The account should have a point of view.

Useful phrases:
- “This is genuinely impressive.”
- “The demo looks great, but...”
- “I’m not convinced this changes much yet.”
- “The interesting part isn’t the benchmark. It’s...”

## Consistency

Use one primary AI voice for the account during the POC unless there is a strong creative reason to change it. The goal is recognizable audio identity.
