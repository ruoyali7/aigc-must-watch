# Content Strategy

## POC Objective

Validate whether English-speaking audiences will consistently watch AI-focused short-form content that combines curation, commentary, humor, and visual storytelling.

## 30-Post POC Mix

- 12 × AI News, But Fun
- 10 × AI Film / AIGC Commentary
- 5 × How They Made This
- 3 × Weekly or Monthly Rankings

Target window: 4–6 weeks.

## Platform Strategy

Create one 9:16 master and publish to:
- TikTok
- YouTube Shorts
- Instagram Reels

Do not blindly duplicate titles/captions across platforms. Repackage metadata for each platform.

## Content Selection Principle

Do not ask only: “Is this important?”

Ask:
- Is it visually explainable?
- Is there a human analogy?
- Is there a meme or cultural metaphor?
- Is there a surprising or emotional beat?
- Will a non-expert care?
- Can the first 3 seconds stop a scroll?

## News Format

Typical 30–60 second structure:

1. 0–3s — Hook
2. 3–10s — Most surprising fact / WTF
3. 10–25s — Context
4. 25–40s — Visual payoff / joke / metaphor
5. 40–52s — Why it matters
6. 52–60s — Punchline or CTA

## AI Film Format

1. Lead with the strongest visual shot
2. Introduce premise only after attention is earned
3. Explain why the work is worth watching
4. Highlight technical or creative execution
5. Give a clear opinion or score
6. Credit the creator and direct viewers to the original work

## Ranking Format

Avoid a generic #10 → #1 countdown when possible. Prefer editorial awards such as:
- Best Story
- Best Visuals
- Best Animation
- Most Unhinged
- Best Commercial
- Biggest WTF
- Film of the Month

## Learning Loop

After each batch, review:
- hook retention
- average percentage viewed
- completion rate
- shares
- saves
- comments
- profile visits
- follows per 1,000 views

Use results to update `POST-SOP.md`, `VOICE-GUIDE.md`, and the scoring rubric.
