## Selection Notes
For every selected work:
- title
- creator
- original URL
- why it won
- strongest clip timestamps
- verified tools if known
- attribution / rights notes

## Episode Structure
1. Cold open with the strongest clip
2. State the edition and promise
3. Move quickly through awards
4. Give each winner one memorable reason
5. Save the strongest emotional or visual payoff for the final award
6. End with a call for submissions / viewer picks when appropriate
