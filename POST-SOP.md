# Post SOP

Use this SOP every time a new post starts. Do not skip directly to scripting unless the story angle and visual concept are already clear.

## Step 1 — Discovery

Build a candidate pool from recent AI news, creator work, competitions, official demos, YouTube/Vimeo/TikTok/Instagram posts, newsletters, Reddit, Hacker News, Product Hunt, and company announcements.

For news, collect roughly 20–30 raw candidates before filtering.
For AI films, maintain an ongoing weekly candidate pool.

Output for each candidate:
- title
- source URL
- date
- one-sentence summary
- category
- why it might matter
- visual opportunities

## Step 2 — Score Candidates

Use `SCORING-RUBRIC.md`.

Score:
- Importance
- Freshness
- Visual Potential
- Humor Potential
- WTF / Surprise Factor
- General Audience Appeal
- Shareability

Select 3–5 finalists. Recommend one, but keep final editorial choice human.

## Step 3 — Find the Angle

Before writing the script, answer:

1. What is actually interesting here?
2. What is the simplest human analogy?
3. What is the joke, tension, surprise, or emotional beat?
4. Why should a non-expert care?
5. What is our actual opinion?

Do not proceed if the angle is only “Company X released Product Y.”

## Step 4 — Design the Visual Concept

For every major sentence, ask:

> What is the funniest, clearest, or most satisfying visual way to communicate this idea?

Choose from:
- official demo / source footage
- creator clip
- meme / internet culture clip
- AI-generated scene
- screen recording
- motion graphic / typography
- diagram / benchmark / number visualization

Draft a shot-by-shot outline with timestamps before editing.

## Step 5 — Meme Matching

Translate the story relationship into a meme situation.

Examples:
- new challenger enters
- comeback
- company rivalry
- product failure
- overhyped launch
- everyone copying
- unexpected winner
- money explosion
- job replacement
- skeptical reaction

Search `library/MEME-LIBRARY.md` and record candidate clips or concepts.

Do not force a meme if it makes the explanation less clear.

## Step 6 — Script

Write for spoken English, not for reading.

Target style:
- smart
- conversational
- lightly sarcastic
- concise
- culturally aware
- opinionated without becoming ranty

Typical news structure:
- 0–3s Hook
- 3–10s WTF / strongest fact
- 10–25s Context
- 25–40s Visual payoff / joke
- 40–52s Why it matters
- 52–60s Punchline / CTA

Every script should answer:
- What happened?
- Why should I care?
- What is the memorable visual or joke?

## Step 7 — Voice

Use a consistent English AI voice identity.

Follow `VOICE-GUIDE.md`.

Before generating final narration:
- shorten long sentences
- insert pauses
- identify emphasized words
- remove corporate phrasing
- make sure the read sounds natural at normal short-form pacing

## Step 8 — Asset Plan

Create an asset checklist before editing:
- source footage
- creator clips
- memes
- screenshots
- logos
- generated scenes
- screen recordings
- charts / graphics
- music / SFX
- attribution text

For third-party clips, record source and usage rationale. Prefer short excerpts used directly for commentary, criticism, or transformation. When practical, use authorized, licensed, remix-enabled, public-domain, or original/generated alternatives.

## Step 9 — Edit

Master format:
- vertical 9:16
- 1080 × 1920

Editing principle:

> Every 1.5–3 seconds, something visually meaningful should change.

That may be:
- cut
- crop / zoom
- text emphasis
- animation
- new footage
- visual reveal
- reaction
- data point

Do not cut merely to create motion. Each change should add information, humor, or energy.

## Step 10 — Captions

Captions must work for muted viewing.

Use emphasis selectively for key words, numbers, names, or punchlines.

Avoid turning the screen into a transcript wall.

## Step 11 — QA

Before publishing, verify:
- hook works without context
- factual claims match current primary/reliable sources
- creator/source attribution is correct
- visuals actually support narration
- no dead section longer than ~3 seconds
- punchline does not distort the underlying fact
- voice is consistent with the brand
- subtitles are readable
- music does not overpower narration
- third-party material is minimized and transformed around commentary

## Step 12 — Publish

Export one clean 9:16 master.

Publish to:
- TikTok
- YouTube Shorts
- Instagram Reels

Customize title/caption/hashtags per platform.

Record:
- publish URL
- publish time
- format
- hook
- topic
- content pillar

## Step 13 — Analytics Review

Capture performance at useful checkpoints such as 24h, 72h, and 7d when possible.

Track:
- views
- 3-second retention / viewed vs swiped if available
- average watch time
- average percentage viewed
- completion rate
- shares
- saves
- comments
- profile visits
- follows

Write one sentence for:
- what worked
- what failed
- what to repeat or change next time

## Daily Working Command

When starting a production day, use this flow:

1. Generate today’s candidate pool.
2. Score and shortlist 3–5 ideas.
3. Recommend one post.
4. After selection, proceed sequentially through Angle → Visual Concept → Meme Match → Script → Voice → Assets → Edit → Publish.
5. Keep a post record in `content/in-production/` until published.
6. Move the record to `content/published/` after publishing and append analytics over time.
