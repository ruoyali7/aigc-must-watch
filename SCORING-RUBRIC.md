# Scoring Rubric

Score each candidate from 1–10 on the dimensions below.

## Dimensions

### Importance
How consequential is the story or work?

### Freshness
How timely is it for the intended post date?

### Visual Potential
Can the idea be shown clearly and memorably rather than merely explained?

### Humor Potential
Is there a natural comedic analogy, reaction, rivalry, absurdity, or cultural reference?

### WTF / Surprise Factor
Will a viewer plausibly think “wait, what?”

### General Audience Appeal
Can someone outside the AI niche understand why this is interesting?

### Shareability
Would someone send this to a friend or repost it?

## Weighted Score

Recommended weighting:
- Importance: 15%
- Freshness: 10%
- Visual Potential: 20%
- Humor Potential: 15%
- WTF / Surprise: 15%
- General Audience Appeal: 15%
- Shareability: 10%

## Decision Guide

- **8.5–10.0:** strong candidate, prioritize
- **7.5–8.4:** good candidate, do if angle is strong
- **6.5–7.4:** only proceed with a distinctive treatment
- **Below 6.5:** usually skip

## Editorial Override

A lower-scoring story can still be selected if it uniquely supports the brand, fills a strategic content gap, or has exceptional creator/industry relevance. Record the reason for the override.
