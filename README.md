# AIGC Must Watch

A working repository for building an English-first AI visual media brand focused on:

- AIGC / AI film curation and rankings
- AI news turned into visual, humorous short-form storytelling
- AI filmmaking breakdowns and tool-focused explainers

## Core Positioning

> We turn what's happening in AI into something worth watching.

The project combines editorial taste, commentary, internet culture, visual storytelling, and AI-assisted production.

## Content Pillars

1. **AI Must-Watch** — curated AI films, shorts, ads, animation, music videos, and standout creator work.
2. **AI News, But Fun** — important AI news explained through visual metaphors, memes, official footage, AI-generated scenes, and concise English commentary.
3. **How The Hell Did They Make This?** — breakdowns of strong AI shots, workflows, models, and creative techniques.

## Operating Principle

Do not optimize for volume first. Optimize for:

- strong hooks
- clear editorial point of view
- visual humor
- shareability
- retention
- repeatable production

The first milestone is a 30-post POC across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels.

## Repo Map

- `BRAND.md` — positioning, audience, voice, editorial identity
- `CONTENT-STRATEGY.md` — content pillars, POC mix, publishing logic
- `POST-SOP.md` — end-to-end production workflow for every post
- `VOICE-GUIDE.md` — English narration personality and writing rules
- `SCORING-RUBRIC.md` — how to score candidate stories and films
- `templates/` — reusable post templates
- `library/` — meme categories and source lists
- `analytics/` — performance tracking and review
- `content/` — candidate, active, and published post records

## Current POC Goal

Publish 30 posts in roughly 4–6 weeks and learn which content format produces the strongest combination of retention, shares, saves, profile visits, and follows.
