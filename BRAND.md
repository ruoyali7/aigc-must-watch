# Brand

## Positioning

**AI × Visual Storytelling × Internet Culture × Commentary**

Core promise:

> We turn what's happening in AI into something worth watching.

Alternative audience-facing line:

> We watch AI so you don't have to. The films. The news. The weird stuff.

## Audience

Primary:
- people who follow AI but do not want to read every article or watch long-form coverage
- AI video creators, filmmakers, designers, and creative technologists
- tech-curious viewers who respond to humor and visual storytelling

Secondary:
- creators looking for tools and workflows
- AI companies and creative software brands
- filmmakers submitting work for discovery

## Editorial Identity

The account should feel like a smart friend with taste, not a corporate newsroom and not an AI content farm.

Desired traits:
- sharp
- conversational
- visually literate
- lightly sarcastic
- culturally aware
- concise
- willing to say when something is overhyped

Avoid:
- generic hype
- robotic summaries
- excessive jargon
- pretending every release is revolutionary
- ranking based on sponsorship

## Content Pillars

### 1. AI Must-Watch
Curated reviews and rankings of standout AI-generated films, shorts, ads, music videos, animation, and visual experiments.

### 2. AI News, But Fun
Important AI developments translated into entertaining visual stories using official footage, memes, cultural references, generated scenes, screen recordings, and motion graphics.

### 3. How The Hell Did They Make This?
Fast breakdowns of notable AI visuals, tools, workflows, and filmmaking techniques.

## Editorial Independence

Sponsored content is allowed, but paid placement must not determine ranking outcomes. Weekly picks, monthly awards, and editorial scores should remain independent.

## Long-Term Direction

Start as an AI-native short-form media account. If the format works, expand into:
- monthly flagship rankings
- film submissions
- newsletter
- website / discovery database
- affiliate tool recommendations
- sponsorships
- original AI visual projects
